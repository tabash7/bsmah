# SEO and editorial linking

The pages target two complementary reader tasks. AlRetaj explains the business problem and shows EMPID screens. Bsmah provides implementation checklists, acceptance criteria, and rollout steps. The articles have separate titles, summaries, bodies, and self-referencing canonical URLs.

Three Bsmah articles link to relevant AlRetaj resources, and one links to an Orkeed article about adoption after a custom app is built. Three AlRetaj articles link to Bsmah, and one links to Orkeed's branch rollout article. Each link is inside an article and describes why the destination helps. The sites do not have sitewide cross-links or one-for-one link exchanges.

Built in: Arabic `lang` and RTL direction, descriptive titles and meta descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, Organization/BlogPosting/Breadcrumb structured data, sitemap, robots file, internal related-article links, responsive images, and descriptive image text. The branded Open Graph image is a 25 KB WebP file.

Before publication, verify ownership and hosting for both domains, serve each site over HTTPS, submit the corresponding sitemap to Search Console, and review indexing and queries there. A local preview cannot establish whether Google has indexed the pages or how they rank. Avoid adding links solely to manufacture ranking signals; keep only links that help a reader continue the task.

Google references: [link best practices](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable), [canonical URL guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls), and [spam policies on excessive link exchanges](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
